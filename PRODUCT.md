# Resep Nurolanda

## Platform
Responsive web. Product requirements live in docs/PRD.md.

## Current scope
Owner-approved animated intro and hero prototype. Visitors will browse Chinese Food and Indonesian Food and contact the business through WhatsApp. Real menu, photos, contacts and approved reviews remain required before production.

## Stack
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Vite, selected for this isolated prototype. Vivus animates the intro's pen strokes; CSS handles the hero transitions. No backend. The PRD suggests Next.js but does not require it.

## Approved direction
Two-line yellow connected handwriting on dark red: finish Resep before writing Nurolanda, then glow. Intro slides up; centered hero enters underneath. Cream hero has a gray text placeholder, two buttons, one large tilted blue food placeholder below, and two smaller delayed placeholders at the edges. Food enters with a quick rise and a small, smooth bounce before settling. Images remain empty at the owner's request. Intro plays once per tab session, with replay and reduced-motion support.
